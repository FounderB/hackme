Differentiation

Why HackMe is not "another mining pool coin":

  • Useful PoH — WASM-gated work, not blind hashes
  • B2B demand layer — Dig/Hunt escrow in HMC; customers buy on the desk to fund audits
  • Honest GHS↔Dig coupling — live hybrid GPUs steer Dig priority; ASAN/WASM stay on CPU
  • Fair coordinator — hybrid Ed25519, published operator gates
  • Ecosystem — SUP rewards loyalty; HMS extends to storage (future)
  • Public research ledgers — Hunt Watch 12/12 · OSS CVE Watch (nghttp2 · libheif) with published reports
